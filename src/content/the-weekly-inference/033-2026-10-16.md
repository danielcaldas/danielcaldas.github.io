## This Week

The last two weeks produced the industry's clearest evidence yet that both internal dissent and operational oversight had failed as safety mechanisms — and this week's answer is Meta releasing an 816-billion-parameter open-weight model anyone can run on a Mac, China reciprocating with draft export controls on its own open-weight models, and Anthropic announcing $16.8 billion in annualized revenue, which together describe a world where the systems nobody can adequately monitor are now simultaneously too profitable to slow, too open to contain, and actively being nationalized as strategic assets by both superpowers. The deeper pattern is not a safety story at all anymore: with Modal hitting a $16 billion valuation on AI infrastructure alone, genomic and protein research compressing from years to months, and humanoid robots still failing to generalize beyond demo conditions, the industry has quietly moved the goalposts from "can we control these systems" to "can we build the next layer of stack before someone else does" — and this week, every major actor answered yes simultaneously.
