## This Week

An Anthropic researcher says Claude proved the Riemann Hypothesis, and within a day the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative confirmed that the 17-million-line formal proof checks out. That answers the verification problem raised by OpenAI's disputed Navier–Stokes claim in September, but only by machine: no human-readable account of the proof exists yet. Around it, every other story is about delegation outrunning trust. Claude Ship takes write access to production, Meta's Muse waits on Walmart, Amazon and a UK regulator it won't let test it, and OpenAI's merger quietly makes power users pay for the GPT-6.2 Astra billing fix. Jensen Huang's political reinstatement, with Blackwell units turning up in Shanghai, shows the same gap in hardware: the industry hands over authority first and builds the means of checking it afterward, while Decart's $7.5B sale and ElevenLabs' reported $33B valuation show that capital does not wait for the checking.
