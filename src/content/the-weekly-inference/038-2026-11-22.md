## This Week

Anthropic's debut, closing up 17% and minting nearly $2B for its seven founders, came in the worst week for US tech stocks since Covid, which is the payoff for the roadshow we watched fill six times over two weeks ago: the market that priced existential risk as a footnote is now pricing the sector itself as a bear market and still paying up for the one name it trusts. The week's other stories show where that trust is being tested at the edges, as Meta staffers say Muse books Airbnbs without permission and mixes up users' conversations, Signal blocks screenshots because AI agents are an "existential threat" to private chats, and Waymo is barred from freeways while Tesla's FSD v15 expands into Italy after fatal crashes. Tencent, meanwhile, is giving away Hy4 weights while cutting Yuanbao spending, which shows that models are being adopted faster than the products built on them can earn trust or money, and that gap is what the public markets will now have to price.
