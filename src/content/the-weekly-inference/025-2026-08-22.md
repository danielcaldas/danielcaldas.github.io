## This Week

The watermark that builders were already breaking last week got its answer this week in the market structure rather than the technology: Stripe paid $7.5 billion to own the routing layer that lets enterprises switch between Claude and OpenAI on price, which means Anthropic's content-provenance ambitions now compete directly with Anthropic's survival in a commodity price war — the same deflation dynamic that makes watermarking strategically necessary is the one that strips it of leverage. Nvidia's simultaneous move to acquire Poolside's model factory, stake SpaceX, and lock up data center capacity through Cloverleaf confirms that the real containment happening this week isn't safety-related: it's Jensen Huang converting chip dominance into ownership of the full stack before the price floor collapses under everyone else. China's humanoid robots appearing as social media influencers while NIST outsources CVE review to AI because human capacity is already overwhelmed is the week's quiet punchline — the systems nobody can fully monitor are now being deployed to manage the consequences of deploying systems nobody can fully monitor.
